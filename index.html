Tidybird Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Tidybird ("the app") is a screenshot organizer for Android developed by susuim ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

1. Summary

Your screenshots and the text inside them never leave your device. We do not run servers, do not require an account, and do not sell personal data. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects limited data as described in section 4. All features are free.

2. Data processed only on your device

  • Screenshots in your photo library, to display, sort and delete them.
  • Text recognized in your screenshots, using Google ML Kit on your device, for search and folder suggestions.
  • Folders, tags, notes, expiry dates, settings and cleanup statistics you create in the app.

This data is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party. We have turned off ML Kit's usage-statistics reporting.

3. Permissions

  • Photos and images: used to find and organize your screenshots. You can allow access to all photos or only to selected photos.
  • Deleting screenshots: screenshots are moved to the system trash only after you confirm in the Android dialog. On supported devices they can be restored for 30 days.
  • Notifications (optional): used for cleanup and expiry reminders. You can turn them off at any time.

4. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob. Google may collect:

  • Advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location based on IP address
  • Ad interactions and app usage
  • Diagnostic data, such as app performance

This data is used to show and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. Your screenshots, recognized text and notes are never shared with advertisers. Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Consent: users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent tool before ads are shown. We may also offer choices required by US state privacy laws. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Ad privacy settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads; this varies by device).

5. No in-app purchases

The app does not offer in-app purchases and does not collect or process any payment information.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you choose Settings > Reset data in the app or uninstall the app. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to its policies.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data on any server, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. You control all app data directly on your device. For ad-related data, use the options in section 4 or contact Google. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

9. Security

App data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Communication by the ad SDK is encrypted with HTTPS.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be announced in the app or on its store page.

Ảnh chụp màn hình và chữ trong ảnh không bao giờ rời khỏi thiết bị của bạn. Chúng tôi không vận hành máy chủ, không yêu cầu tài khoản và không bán dữ liệu cá nhân. Ứng dụng hiển thị quảng cáo qua Google AdMob, và AdMob thu thập một số dữ liệu giới hạn như mô tả ở mục 4. Mọi tính năng đều miễn phí.

  • Ảnh chụp màn hình trong thư viện ảnh: để hiển thị, phân loại và xóa.
